Finding 01 Direct, hands-on support overcame the initial hesitation that a one-off workshop or pamphlet never could.
Finding 02 Follow-up visits were essential — habits introduced on day one rarely survived the week without a second check-in.
Finding 03 Interventions worked best when they matched each owner's existing record-keeping habits instead of replacing them outright.
