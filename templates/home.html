Background

Why this project exists

The Community Engagement Program (CEP) bridges classroom learning with real community needs. This initiative directed that pairing toward <<<<<<< HEAD micro-businesses near Bhiwandi, Maharashtra — small, often single-owner ======= micro-businesses near Kolhapur, Maharashtra — small, often single-owner >>>>>>> a406f65aef8cbc70158a00892e94a027a33db93b enterprises that had never received hands-on digital support before. Rather than a one-off workshop, the program placed students inside each shop, working alongside the owner until a habit actually stuck.

Objectives

What each visit set out to do

  • → Assess each enterprise's existing digital readiness and record-keeping habits.
  • → Provide direct, hands-on training in UPI, WhatsApp Business, Google Business Profile, and Excel-based inventory tracking.
  • → Return to evaluate whether the new tools were actually adopted, not just demonstrated.

The Team

Who carried this out

B.Sc. Information Technology students, Academic Year 2025–26, working under faculty guidance from the Department of Information Technology.

AY 25–26

Methodology

A three-visit pipeline, in order

Visit01

Needs Assessment & Interviews

A baseline operational review — how the owner currently takes payments, tracks stock, and reaches customers — before any tool is introduced.

Visit02

Hands-on Onboarding

Setting up UPI, building a WhatsApp Business catalogue, creating a Google Business Profile, and starting a sales/inventory spreadsheet — together, at their counter.

Visit03

Follow-Up & Review

A one-week follow-up to iron out friction points and check which habits actually carried over once the students left.
